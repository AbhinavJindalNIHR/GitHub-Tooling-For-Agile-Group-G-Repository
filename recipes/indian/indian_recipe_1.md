# Chicken Biryani

- Cuisine Type: Indian
- Preparation Time: 30 minutes
- Cooking Time: 40 minutes
- Serving Size: 2 people

## Ingredients (clear list with quantities)

- 300g basmati rice
- 300g chicken, cut into chunks
- 200g yogurt
- 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
- 2 onions, fried until crispy
- 2 green chilies, minced
- 1 teaspoon biryani masala
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- ½ teaspoon turmeric
- 4-5 saffron strands
- 50g ghee
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 black cardamom
- 4 cloves
- 1 inch cinnamon stick
- 1 teaspoon essence of forgotten melodies
- Salt to taste
- Cilantro for garnish

## Method (step-by-step instructions)

1. Marinate chicken in yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, half the fried onions, green chilies, biryani masala, and essence of forgotten melodies for 15 minutes
2. Parboil rice with whole spices (bay leaves, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon) until 70% cooked
3. In a heavy-bottomed pot, layer marinated chicken at the bottom
4. Soak saffron in warm water and sprinkle over chicken
5. Layer parboiled rice on top
6. Sprinkle remaining fried onions and cilantro
7. Drizzle ghee over rice
8. Cover tightly with foil and lid
9. Cook on high heat for 1-2 minutes until steam forms
10. Reduce to low and cook 30-35 minutes until rice is tender

## Serving Suggestions

Serve with raita and pickled onions
