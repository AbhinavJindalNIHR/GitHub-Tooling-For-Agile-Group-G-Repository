# Black Hole Gravity Reduction Sauce with Event Horizon Jus

## info
* About 8 hours (or eternities, impossible to measure)
* Serves infinite dimension

## ingredients
* 2 cups matter compressed beyond recognition
* 1 cup gravitational force extract (harvested at infinite density)
* 1/2 cup event horizon reduction
* 3 tbsp spacetime distortion oil
* 2 large onions collapsed into singularity
* 6 cloves garlic achieving gravitational collapse
* 4 cups stock that escapes observation
* 1 tbsp ground singularity dust
* 1 tsp hawking radiation seasoning
* 1/4 cup melted time itself
* Fresh spaghettified herb garnish
* Tidal force foam topping
* Garnish: fragments of matter torn apart by differential gravity

## steps
1. In a pot that may itself become a small black hole, heat spacetime oil
2. Add onions that will collapse into point masses
3. Add garlic achieving gravitational singularity
4. Watch both ingredients emit Hawking radiation as they cook
5. Carefully pour in stock that bends light
6. Add compressed matter—it will distort all nearby space
7. The mixture will curve the fabric of spacetime around it
8. Pour in event horizon reduction—anything added past this point cannot be retrieved
9. Add gravitational force extract using specialized quantum utensils
10. The sauce will develop its own event horizon
11. Season with singularity dust and Hawking radiation
12. Stir with a spoon that is simultaneously inside and outside the pot
13. Slowly reduce for 8 hours while gravity increases infinitely
14. Melt time itself into the sauce—causality becomes optional
15. Add spaghettified herbs (they will be stretched infinitely thin)
16. Top with tidal force foam that pulls in different directions simultaneously
17. Garnish with fragments torn apart by differential gravity
18. Serve on plates that orbit the sauce rather than hold it
19. Consumption will slow your time perception relative to diners
20. Warning: Sauce may consume your restaurant's location permanently
